CAMERA MOVEMENT:

Camera only moves when player is 20 % close to either edge, AND there is more world to explore.
In order to draw the different props in the world, the camera offset is subtracted from
the prop original positions.

E.g.:

background.rect.x - camera.offset_x

This meaning:
Left edge of background - how many pixels the camera has scrolled = where to draw background

This would take the background x position (left edge of background) and subtract the camera
offset.
Let's say background started at position 0. Now camera has moved 200 pixels to the right.
Then background x position will be drawn at x = -200, so the background appears to move to the left.

Another important point is the difference between player world position and player screen position.

For simplicity, I have made the world pretty straightforward.

My window is:
640 x 480
And my world is:
1280 x 480

So the world is twice as big as the window.

If player is at position x = 800, this means IN THE WORLD.
Cause the player can't be at position x = 800 on the screen, because the window is only 640
pixels wide.

This means that the real position of the player on the window will be:
player_window_pos_x = player_world_pos_x - camera.offset_x
